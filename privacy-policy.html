Privacy Policy

What data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Last updated: January 1, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Deskfix ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit deskfix.blog. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to being transparent about our practices.

Information we collect

Analytics data

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. This collects anonymous data including pages visited, time spent on site, device type, browser, and approximate location (country/city level). This data is aggregated and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Newsletter subscriptions

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and optionally your first name. This information is stored and processed by Kit (formerly ConvertKit). We use it only to send you the newsletter you signed up for. We never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties.

Contact form submissions

If you use our contact form, your name, email address, and message are processed by Formspree and forwarded to our email. We use this information only to reply to your message and do not retain it beyond that purpose.

Cookies

Our site uses cookies primarily for Google Analytics. Affiliate links to Amazon may also set cookies as part of the Amazon Associates programme. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to understand how our content is being used and to improve the site, to send our newsletter to subscribers who have opted in, and to reply to contact form messages. We do not use your data for advertising, do not sell it to third parties, and do not use it for any purpose beyond what is stated here.

Third party services

This site uses the following third party services, each of which has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Analytics — website analytics. Google Privacy Policy
  • Kit (ConvertKit) — newsletter delivery. Kit Privacy Policy
  • Formspree — contact form processing. Formspree Privacy Policy
  • Amazon Associates — affiliate programme. Amazon Privacy Policy

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of any personal data we hold about you. To unsubscribe from our newsletter, use the unsubscribe link in any email we send. For any other data requests, contact us and we will respond within 30 days.

Data retention

Newsletter subscriber data is retained for as long as you remain subscribed. Contact form data is not retained beyond the initial reply. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics default settings (26 months).

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent update. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, get in touch.
